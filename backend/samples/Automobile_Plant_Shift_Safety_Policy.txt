# APEX AUTOMOTIVE MANUFACTURING CORP.
## Standard Operating Policy: Plant Shift Schedules, Attendance & Shop Floor Safety Protocols
Document ID: APEX-HR-SOP-2026-01
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Target Audience: All Plant Operations, Assembly Technicians, Shop Floor Supervisors, Quality Control Inspectors

### 1. Shift Structure and Timings
Apex Automotive operates on a 3-shift rotational model across all assembly, stamping, paint shop, and powertrain units:
- Shift A (Morning Shift): 06:00 AM – 02:30 PM (includes 30-minute lunch and two 15-minute ergonomics breaks)
- Shift B (Evening Shift): 02:30 PM – 11:00 PM (includes 30-minute dinner and two 15-minute ergonomics breaks)
- Shift C (Night / Maintenance Shift): 11:00 PM – 06:30 AM (includes 30-minute midnight refreshment break)
Shift Rotation: Assembly line teams rotate between Shift A and Shift B every two weeks. Shift C is staffed by dedicated night operations teams and rotating maintenance technicians with a 15% night shift differential allowance.

### 2. Punctuality and Biometric Attendance Rules
1. Punch-in Grace Period: Employees are permitted a grace period of 7 minutes after shift commencement. Arriving after 7 minutes is marked as 'Late Arrival'.
2. Three consecutive Late Arrivals within a calendar month result in deduction of half-day casual leave.
3. Unannounced Absence: Any absence exceeding 2 consecutive days without notifying the Shift Supervisor and HR Plant Desk via the HR Portal is classified as Unauthorized Absence (AWOL) and initiates disciplinary review.

### 3. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Compliance
Safety is non-negotiable across all automotive assembly zones:
- Mandatory Standard PPE: Steel-toed safety boots (EN ISO 20345 certified), anti-cut Kevlar gloves (Level 4 or 5 depending on sheet metal handling), and high-visibility reflective vests.
- Paint Shop & Chemical Zone: High-efficiency particulate air (HEPA) respirators with organic vapor cartridges, nitrile gloves, and chemical splash goggles.
- Stamping & Heavy Press Lines: Custom-molded hearing protection (minimum NRR 28 dB) and polycarbonate impact face shields.
Non-compliance penalty: First violation triggers a formal safety counseling note; repeat violation within 90 days results in a 3-day suspension without pay.

### 4. Machine Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) Protocol
Prior to any maintenance, clearing jammed robotic arm cells, or tooling replacement, technicians must apply personalized LOTO padlocks with danger tags.
Only the designated technician whose key matches the lockout has authority to remove the tag upon job verification.

### 5. Heat and Heavy Labor Fatigue Management
During summer operations (temperatures exceeding 35°C in press or forge units), mandatory 10-minute hydration intervals are enforced every 90 minutes. Electrolyte hydration stations and cooling lounges are accessible adjacent to all assembly bays.
