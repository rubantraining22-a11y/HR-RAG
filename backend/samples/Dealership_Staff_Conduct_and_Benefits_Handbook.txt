# APEX AUTOMOTIVE RETAIL & DEALERSHIP NETWORK
## Employee Conduct, Sales Incentives & Health Benefits Handbook
Document ID: APEX-RETAIL-HB-2026-08
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Target Audience: Showroom Sales Consultants, Service Advisors, Customer Experience Managers, F&I Officers

### 1. Showroom Professional Grooming & Customer Engagement Standards
1. Attire: Professional corporate formal attire or Apex-branded business suits. Name badges must be worn on the left lapel at all times during customer-facing hours.
2. Vehicle Test Drive Protocol:
   - Sales Consultants must verify customer driver's license authenticity and signed liability waiver before any test drive commences.
   - Pre-designated GPS geofenced routes must be followed for all test drives.
   - Speeding, aggressive vehicle acceleration, or using mobile devices during test drives is strictly grounds for immediate termination.

### 2. Dealership Sales Incentive & Commission Framework
1. New Vehicle Delivery Incentives: Tiered bonuses structured on monthly unit volume:
   - Tier 1 (1–8 units delivered): $150 per vehicle.
   - Tier 2 (9–15 units delivered): $275 per vehicle retroactively applied from unit 1.
   - Tier 3 (16+ units delivered): $400 per vehicle plus President's Club quarterly recognition bonus.
2. Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) Multiplier: Sales consultants maintaining a 5-star CSI rating (minimum 94% customer survey satisfaction) receive a 1.25x multiplier on their monthly earned commission pool.

### 3. Healthcare, Wellness & Vehicle Discount Perks
1. Comprehensive Medical Insurance: Apex covers 100% of employee premium and 75% of dependent family coverage, including outpatient, dental ($1,500/yr), and optical ($400 every two years).
2. Employee Vehicle Purchase Program (EVPP):
   - Permanent employees with 6+ months tenure can purchase brand-new Apex vehicles at Dealer Invoice Cost minus 6% employee discount.
   - Limit: Up to 2 vehicles per employee per calendar year (for self or immediate household family members).
   - Low-interest captive automotive financing at 1.99% APR via Apex Financial Services.
3. Fuel & Mobility Allowance: Service Advisors and Field Consultants receive a monthly fuel and transit card loaded with $220.
